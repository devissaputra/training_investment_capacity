# Released Results

The release separates three dimensions of employer training capacity:

1. training reach;
2. real investment intensity;
3. sector distribution.

## Long-run result

Real UK training spend per employee:

```text
£2,410 in 2011 → £1,700 in 2024
```

Released real change:

**−29.46%**

Real spend per trainee fell **38.69%** and total real training expenditure fell **18.53%** over the same endpoints.

## 2022–2024 divergence

- derived reach: 60.22% → 62.85%
- real spend per employee: £1,960 → £1,700
- real spend per trainee: £3,250 → £2,710

Reach and investment intensity therefore moved in different directions.

This is not interpreted as improved efficiency because the dataset does not measure training outcomes.

## 2024 sector distribution

- highest spend per employee: Construction, £2,630
- lowest: Public admin., £920
- max/min ratio: 2.86×
- unweighted median: £1,570
- unweighted CV: 0.2623
