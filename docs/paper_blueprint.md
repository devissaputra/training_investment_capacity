# Paper Blueprint

## Working title
Employer Training Investment Capacity: A Longitudinal UK Study

## Motivation
L&D capacity is not only about how many people receive training. It also concerns the real resources employers deploy per worker and trainee. The UK Employer Skills Survey provides a long-running official series that allows changes in reach to be compared with changes in expenditure intensity.

## Research question
How have real employer training investment intensity and training reach changed over time, and how heterogeneous is 2024 spending across sectors?

## Data and method
Use official UK-wide Investment in Training estimates for 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, 2022, and 2024. The 2019 wave is excluded from the UK time series because Scotland did not participate. Use the source-provided 2024-price expenditure measures, derive the trainees/employees ratio, and compare all 13 2024 sectors.

## Results to report
Real spend per employee fell from £2,410 to £1,700 between 2011 and 2024 (−29.46%). Real spend per trainee fell 38.69% and total real expenditure fell 18.53% over the same period. The derived reach ratio rose 8.26 percentage points from 2011 and 2.63 points from 2022. In 2024, spend per employee ranged from £920 in Public admin. to £2,630 in Construction, a 2.86× max/min ratio.

Report the complete six-wave national table and all 13 sector rows rather than cherry-picking only endpoints.

## Robustness / sensitivity
The release triangulates the investment-intensity story using per-employee, per-trainee, and total expenditure. Sector heterogeneity is summarized with range, max/min ratio, unweighted median, and unweighted coefficient of variation. No sampling confidence interval is invented from an aggregate table that does not provide the required variance information.

## Survey-method limitation
The 2024 Investment in Training results are based on a follow-up survey. DfE reports 6,210 expenditure interviews, of which 5,935 were retained after incomplete cases were removed. Published expenditure estimates also include documented modelling of missing survey components. A paper should cite the official methodology directly and avoid treating the aggregate table as raw accounting data.

## Construct limitation
Observed expenditure intensity and training reach are indicators of L&D resource deployment. They do not measure training quality, causal returns, individual learning, or latent organizational capacity.

## Publication integrity
Do not describe this repository as peer reviewed, preregistered, causal, or externally validated unless those events occur. Distinguish official published estimates from repository-derived calculations.
