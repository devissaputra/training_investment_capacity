# References and Data Sources

## Canonical official statistics release

Department for Education / Skills England. *Employer Skills Survey 2024*. Explore Education Statistics. Published 24 July 2025.

The release provides official statistics on vacancies, skills gaps, training, and employer investment in training.

## Investment in Training dataset

Department for Education / Skills England. *Investment in training*. Dataset from Employer Skills Survey 2024.

The dataset contains 7,848 rows, covers 2011 to 2024, and provides total investment, spend per employee, spend per trainee, employee counts, trainee counts, sector, site size, and geographic breakdowns.

Dataset page:

`https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/data-catalogue/data-set/a07479d1-e26d-4b66-9ec9-e683917c1388`

## 2024 UK research report

Department for Education / Skills England. *Employer skills survey 2024: UK findings*. Published 27 November 2025 and updated 18 June 2026.

The report confirms:

- total 2024 training expenditure of £53.0bn;
- spend per trainee of approximately £2,710;
- spend per employee of approximately £1,700;
- long-run real declines in all three measures;
- sector differences in investment intensity.

## Methodology

Department for Education / Skills England. *Employer Skills Survey 2022–2024: methodology*.

The methodology documents:

- the separate Investment in Training follow-up;
- 6,210 achieved expenditure interviews in 2024;
- exclusion of 275 incomplete interviews;
- 5,935 retained sites;
- weighting;
- modelling of missing expenditure components.

## 2019 comparability

The released UK longitudinal analysis excludes ESS 2019 because Scotland did not participate.

## Reuse

The Explore Education Statistics release provides downloadable government open data. Reuse should follow the publisher's Open Government Licence terms and release guidance.

## How the sources are used

The official dataset supplies the empirical values.

The UK report supplies authoritative interpretation of headline trends.

The methodology supplies survey-production and validity context.

The repository contributes a secondary L&D analysis separating reach, real investment intensity, and sector heterogeneity.
