## Methodological notes used in this repository
The DfE methodology is the authority for the UK-wide comparability decision, the exclusion of 2019 from UK time-series comparisons, the 2024 follow-up sample disposition, and modelling of missing expenditure components. Repository-derived calculations are clearly separated from those official methodological statements.
