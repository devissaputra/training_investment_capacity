# Research Package Definition

This repository qualifies as a Learning and Development research package because it connects an explicit workforce-development question to an official empirical source, a fixed comparability rule, complete derived evidence, descriptive robustness diagnostics, executable source verification, tests, CI, scientific figures, and bounded interpretation.

## Core question

How have real employer training investment intensity and training reach changed over time, and how heterogeneous is 2024 spending across sectors?

## Empirical core

- six comparable UK survey waves;
- source-provided 2024-price expenditure measures;
- derived training-reach ratio;
- complete 13-sector 2024 comparison;
- descriptive robustness diagnostics.

## Main result

Real spend per employee fell from £2,410 in 2011 to £1,700 in 2024, a 29.46% decline.

From 2022 to 2024, the derived training-reach ratio rose from 0.6022 to 0.6285 while real spend per employee fell from £1,960 to £1,700.

## Why this matters for L&D

Reach and investment intensity are different dimensions.

A wider training footprint does not imply greater resource intensity, and lower resource intensity does not by itself establish lower training quality.
